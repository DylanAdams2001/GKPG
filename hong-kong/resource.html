03  ·  The Exact Process

From Registering Interest To First Rent Payment

Exactly what happens, in order, if you decide to move forward. Typically 30 to 60 days start to finish.

  1. Day 1Register your interestSubmit your details and we'll follow up with current pricing, availability and this guide.
  2. Day 1 to 3Discovery callA short, no-obligation call at a time that works for your time zone, to answer any questions this guide didn't cover.
  3. Day 3 to 7ReservationOnce you've chosen a unit, you reserve it with a reservation deposit, taking it off the market while contracts are prepared.
  4. Day 7 to 10Instruct a solicitorYou appoint a solicitor to act for you, either your own or one from a panel we can recommend. You're never obligated to use a panel firm.
  5. Day 10 to 20Due diligenceYour solicitor carries out standard property due diligence on your behalf.
  6. Day 20 to 30Exchange of contractsContracts are formally exchanged, the agreed deposit is paid, and a completion date is set.
  7. Day 30 to 45CompletionThe remaining balance is paid in cash and legal ownership transfers to you. Your name goes on the title deeds.
  8. Day 30 to 45Tenant sourcing beginsSince this is a completed, ready-to-let unit, our management team starts sourcing a tenant as soon as your purchase completes.
  9. Day 30 to 60Your income beginsYou start receiving monthly rental income once a tenant is in place, with regular reporting from that point on.

Timings shown are typical estimates and will vary case by case.

Nothing in this process skips a step you'd expect from any UK property purchase: cash purchase, solicitor-led exchange and completion, title deeds in your name. The difference is what happens after completion, our management partner takes over the operational side rather than you finding and managing a tenant yourself.

05  ·  Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I mortgage the property?

No, this is a cash purchase only. There's no mortgage option on this opportunity, which simplifies the process considerably for overseas buyers.

Is the property fully managed?

Yes. Tenant sourcing, rent collection and day-to-day management are handled on your behalf as part of the service, so you don't need to be in the UK, or hands-on, to hold the investment.

Is the yield really 12% net?

Yes. The 12% figure is a net yield, calculated after all costs and expenses, not a headline gross number.

How long until I can receive a return on investment?

Typically 30 to 60 days from reservation to your first rental payment. Since this is a completed development rather than off-plan, our management team starts sourcing a tenant as soon as your purchase completes, so income begins as quickly as the letting process allows rather than waiting on construction.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. This guide is for general information only. It isn't personal financial advice and nothing here should be treated as a recommendation. We'd always encourage independent financial and legal advice before committing any capital.
