Run the numbers yourself

Put a purchase price and a contracted rent in, and this works out what you would actually need at completion and what the property would actually pay you — with Stamp Duty and every acquisition cost included, not just the headline yield.

Why the headline yield is never the whole story. A yield divides rent by the purchase price. It ignores Stamp Duty, legal fees and, if you are borrowing, the cost of arranging the loan. Those are real pounds you have to find, and they belong in the return. This calculator puts them back in.

How the Stamp Duty is worked out

Buying through a limited company means the higher rates for additional dwellings apply from the first pound — there is no nil-rate band. It is the second largest cheque you write after the deposit, so it is worth seeing in full. Your solicitor will confirm the figure for your purchase.

If the valuation comes in low

Worth understanding before you commit. Supported housing sits between standard buy-to-let and commercial lending, and surveyors often value it on bricks and mortar rather than fully crediting the contracted income. A valuation below the price does not change what you pay — it changes what the lender will advance, and the difference has to come from you in cash. We would rather you saw that here than at the point of exchange.
