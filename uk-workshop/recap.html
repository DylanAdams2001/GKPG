If you only take three things

1
The rent is contracted, not hoped for

The property is let to a registered housing association on a 25 year agreement. The rent is agreed upfront, funded through Housing Benefit for supported accommodation, and rises annually with CPI. It does not depend on a tenant paying.

2
The headaches move to someone else

Voids, arrears, maintenance, re-letting and every tenant call sit with the housing association under the agreement. You own the house. You are not the one running it.

3
The same money buys two houses, not one

A three-bed semi at £325,000 pays £54,600 a year. Buy it cash and you're in for £349,500. Use that same money as deposits instead and it covers two financed houses paying £68,250 a year combined, with £93,450 still in your account.
